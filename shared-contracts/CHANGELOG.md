### Added

- `fleet_status.proto` - FleetStatus schema
- `ai_decision.proto` - AIDecisionLog schema
- `commands.proto` - Strategic command definitions
- `constraints.proto` - Safety constraint definitions
- Safety boundary YAML files (thermal, power, structural, operational)
